# Klino Booking Experience

## Goal

Make booking a cleaner fast, simple, transparent, and secure.

---

# Step 1 — Select Service

The customer chooses a service:

- Home Cleaning
- Deep Cleaning
- Office Cleaning
- Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning
- Window Cleaning
- Post-Construction Cleaning

Button:

[Continue]

---

# Step 2 — Property Details

Customer provides:

- Property type
  - Apartment
  - House
  - Office
  - Shop

- Number of rooms

- Number of bathrooms

- Approximate property size (optional)

[Continue]

---

# Step 3 — Upload Photos

Customer uploads photos of the areas to be cleaned.

Purpose:

- Better price estimates
- Better preparation for agents

Optional:

Customer can add notes.

Example:

"The kitchen needs deep cleaning."

---

# Step 4 — Location

Customer enters:

- Address
- Landmark
- City

Optional:

Use current location.

---

# Step 5 — Date & Time

Customer selects:

- Preferred date
- Preferred arrival time

---

# Step 6 — Price Estimate

Klino provides:

- Estimated cleaning cost
- Platform fee (if applicable)
- Total amount

Customer chooses:

[Accept Quote]

or

[Cancel]

---

# Step 7 — Payment

Customer pays a deposit or the full amount.

Payment status:

- Pending
- Paid

Funds are held securely until the job is completed.

---

# Step 8 — Agent Assignment

Customer receives:

- Agent name
- Profile photo
- Rating
- Years of experience
- Estimated arrival time

---

# Step 9 — Cleaning

Agent:

- Arrives
- Uploads before photos
- Performs cleaning
- Uploads after photos

---

# Step 10 — Completion

Customer reviews the work.

Options:

- Confirm Completion
- Report an Issue

---

# Step 11 — Review

Customer rates:

★★★★★

Leaves an optional review.

Booking is complete.
