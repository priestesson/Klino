# Klino Typography System

## Purpose

Define the fonts, text sizes, and hierarchy used across the Klino website and future applications.

The goal is to make Klino feel:

- Modern
- Professional
- Easy to read
- Trustworthy

---

# Font Style

## Primary Font

Recommended:

- Inter
- Poppins
- Roboto

Reason:

These fonts are:
- Clean
- Modern
- Easy to read on phones and computers

---

# Text Hierarchy

## Heading 1 (H1)

Purpose:

Main page titles.

Examples:

"Trusted Cleaning Services At Your Fingertips"

Style:

- Large
- Bold
- Strong emphasis

---

## Heading 2 (H2)

Purpose:

Section titles.

Examples:

"Why Choose Klino?"

"How It Works"

Style:

- Bold
- Clear

---

## Heading 3 (H3)

Purpose:

Card titles and smaller sections.

Examples:

"Deep Cleaning"

"Verified Agent"

---

## Body Text

Purpose:

Descriptions and information.

Examples:

"Book professional cleaners safely and easily."

Style:

- Easy reading
- Comfortable spacing

---

## Button Text

Purpose:

Actions.

Examples:

- Book Cleaning
- Continue
- Confirm Payment
- Become an Agent

Style:

- Short
- Clear
- Action focused

---

# Text Rules

## Keep It Simple

Avoid complicated words.

Instead of:

"Initiate a service reservation"

Use:

"Book a cleaning"

---

## Build Trust

Use language that communicates:

- Safety
- Verification
- Reliability
- Transparency

---

## Mobile First

Text must remain readable on:

- Phones
- Tablets
- Computers
